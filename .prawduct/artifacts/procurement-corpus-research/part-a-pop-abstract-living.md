Copyright shorthand:
- **EU**: the author's life + 70 years, so the work enters the public domain on 1 January of the death year + 71.
- **US, pre-1978 works**: up to 95 years from publication. Whether a given work was "published" with notice, which is the 1909-Act question, is **not assessed** here, so these are treated as in copyright.
- **US, post-1977 works**: life + 70.

## Per-artist notes

### Robert Rauschenberg (1925–2008)
- **Foundation and catalogue**: the Robert Rauschenberg Foundation's online artwork pages carry full captions (title, date, medium, dimensions, holder, RRF number, e.g. Monogram RRF 59.024). Its catalogue raisonné is a project in progress (https://www.rauschenbergfoundation.org/art/artwork/monogram ; "Catalogue Raisonné Project" in the site nav).
- **Image policy**: the Fair Use Policy (https://www.rauschenbergfoundation.org/foundation/fair-use-policy) recognises fair use for "Analytical, interpretive, and/or creative writings/works", teaching, online collections, news, transformative art and non-profit social media, but "does not include advertising, promotional, and commercial uses of Rauschenberg's artwork (unless previously granted by the Rauschenberg Foundation)". Its contact is fairuse@rauschenbergfoundation.org. It was the first artist foundation to adopt such a policy.
- **Rights**: © Robert Rauschenberg Foundation, which owns the copyright.
- **Alternates**: Canyon Q19887473 (3D Combine with a stuffed eagle); Rebus Q19887272.

### Andy Warhol (1928–1987)
- **Foundation and catalogue**: the Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts holds the copyrights. Its FAQ (https://warholfoundation.org/about/faq/) says to contact "Artists Rights Society (ARS) at info@arsny.com" for high-resolution images. The foundation "does not offer opinions on works of art purported to be by Andy Warhol"; its authentication board ceased in 2011.
- **Catalogue raisonné**: print only (paintings/sculpture/drawings; prints = Feldman & Schellmann). **No online catalogue raisonné found.**
- **Rights**: ARS, New York, is the US rights representative.

### Roy Lichtenstein (1923–1997)
- **Online catalogue raisonné**: free, at https://www.lichtensteincatalogue.org/ (RLCR numbers, e.g. Whaam! RLCR 808, Drowning Girl RLCR 761).
- **Terms** (https://www.lichtensteincatalogue.org/section/?id=Rights+Reproductions): the licence is "personal, non-commercial, educational and research" only, and users "may not (i) download, or attempt to download, any reproduction or image of any work of art".
- **Rights**: "Copyright for most Roy Lichtenstein artworks currently resides with the Estate of Roy Lichtenstein but some reside with the Roy Lichtenstein Foundation"; both are managed by the Estate. The foundation also runs https://lichtensteinfoundation.org/rights-reproductions/.

### Cy Twombly (1928–2011)
- **Foundation**: the Cy Twombly Foundation site is https://cytwombly.org (relaunched 2024). Its catalogues raisonnés (paintings, drawings in 8 vols, sculpture, prints) are **print**.
- **Policy** (https://cytwombly.org/the-foundation): "The Cy Twombly Foundation retains all rights, including copyright, in all paintings, drawings, sculptures, and graphic works", "permission must be obtained directly from us", and "the Foundation grants one-time permissions only" (info@cytwombly.org).
- **Rights**: the Fondazione Nicola Del Roscio holds the rights to Twombly's photographs.

### Ellsworth Kelly (1923–2015)
- **Foundation**: https://ellsworthkelly.org/. Its catalogue raisonné of paintings, reliefs and sculpture (Yve-Alain Bois; vol. 1 1940–53 published 2015, vol. 2 1954–58 published 2021) is listed on https://ellsworthkelly.org/catalogue-raisonne/ **but is not browsable online there**. One search snippet claimed it was digitised (possibly via the Cahiers d'Art Institute); unverified.
- **Rights**: rights requests go via https://ellsworthkelly.org/request-form/ (rights@ekelly.org); credit "© Ellsworth Kelly Foundation". No stated policy text beyond that.

### Agnes Martin (1912–2004)
- **Online catalogue raisonné**: by Artifex Press with the Agnes Martin Catalogue Raisonné (Tiffany Bell): paintings 2017, works on paper 2019, about 1,500 high-resolution images with zoom. It is now offered **by subscription** via the Cahiers d'Art Institute (https://www.cahiersdartinstitute.org/pages/agnes-martin). Image-use terms were not found.
- **Rights**: credits read "© Agnes Martin / Artists Rights Society (ARS), New York" (per search snippets), so ARS administers.

### Gerhard Richter (b. 1932)

### Banksy (pseudonymous)
- **Website and policy**: https://www.banksy.co.uk/licensing.html says: "Only Pest Control Office have permission to use or license my artwork. If someone else has granted you permission, you don't have permission. I wrote 'copyright is for losers' in my (copyrighted) book and still encourage anybody to take and amend my art for their own personal amusement, but not for profit or making it look like I've endorsed something when I haven't."
- **Authentication**: **Pest Control Office** (pestcontroloffice.com) is the sole issuer of certificates of authenticity. Street pieces are generally not certified; the policy on stickers, posters and the like came from secondary sources and was not verified.
- **Edition alternate**: *Girl with Balloon*, screenprint 2004 (ed. 150 unsigned + 88 signed, Pictures on Walls; inferred). It shares its identity with #27 and with the mural Q22000063.
