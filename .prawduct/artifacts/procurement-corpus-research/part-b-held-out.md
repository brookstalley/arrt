## B1: established held-out artists (20)

Every QID was found with `wbsearchentities` and checked with `wbgetentities` on 2026-10-03. The check confirmed P31 = Q5 (human), at least one artist occupation in P106 (painter Q1028181, sculptor Q1281618, artist Q483501, visual artist Q3391743, etc.), and the birth and death years shown. The "movement" values come from Wikidata P135 on the same fetch. The "Near / reason" column is curator judgement unless a source is cited in brackets.

Copyright: **EU** is life + 70, so a work is in the public domain from 1 January of the year death + 71. On 2026-10-03, artists who died in 1955 or earlier are PD. **US** depends on publication date, not death. Works published in 1930 or earlier are PD in 2026. Later works are generally still protected. Unpublished works are PD only if the author died before 1956. The US column therefore applies a death-year rule plus the 1930 publication line, and it is (inferred) for any individual work.

## B2: living, gallery-represented and emerging artists (14)

- **Artlogic CMS**: the page carries a "Site by Artlogic" credit or an "Artlogic CMS" marker.
- **exhibit-E**: no Artlogic credit, but images are served from `static-assets.artlogic.net/…/exhibit-e/…`. Artlogic merged with exhibit-E in January 2022 [S20]. These sites run a different template family. Whether one scraper covers both is (inferred) and untested.

The Wikidata column records `wbsearchentities` followed by `wbgetentities`, with P31 = Q5 and an artist P106 confirmed. "none" means no matching artist item was found under this spelling. It does not prove that no item exists.

## Artlogic count

I tested every gallery site visited for B2 where the platform could be determined. The count is 18 sites: 14 representing galleries plus Petzel, Marianne Boesky, albertz benda and Kordansky. Markel and Nüart are among the 14.

Counting only the one listed representing gallery per B2 artist (14 sites): Artlogic CMS is 5 of 14, and the Artlogic family is 8 of 14.

Caveats:
- **The sample is biased.** Both of Stephens's galleries were chosen because they are his, and both are Artlogic.
- **Sites were dropped** if they were unreachable or had changed: Hionas (no response), Turner Carroll (403), and Elizabeth Harris (the domain is now a spam site; gallery presumably closed, inferred).
- **Some sites block non-browser clients.** David Richard and Morgan Lehman answered only to a browser User-Agent.
- **One scraper may not cover the family.** A scraper keyed on the Artlogic CMS URL pattern (`/artists/<id>-<slug>/overview/`) would reach 7 of 18. Whether exhibit-E's different templates (`/artists/<slug>`) are covered by the same scraper is (inferred) and untested.
