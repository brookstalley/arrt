# Procurement corpus — batch A1 (Delaunays, Exter, Taeuber-Arp, Escher, Sage, Dalí, Calder)

Researched 2026-10-03. Identity and facts only: no image source (Commons, AIC, museum open access, Wikidata P18) was consulted or recorded.

QIDs were checked with `wbgetentities` for P31 (instance of), P170 (creator), P195 (collection), P571 (inception), P186 (material) and P2048/P2049 (height/width). Artist QIDs: Robert Delaunay Q33978 (d. 1941), Sonia Delaunay Q232972 (d. 1979), Aleksandra Ekster/Exter Q242121 (d. 1949), Sophie Taeuber-Arp Q254395 (d. 1943), M. C. Escher Q1470 (d. 1972), Kay Sage Q444567 (d. 1963), Salvador Dalí Q5577 (d. 1989), Alexander Calder Q151580 (d. 1976).

**Copyright basis.** US: works published before 1931 are PD as of 2026. For a foreign work published 1931 or later, I applied URAA restoration, because every foreign artist here was still in copyright at home on 1 Jan 1996, giving publication + 95 years. For US artists (Sage, Calder), a post-1930 work's status depends on notice and renewal. And for a unique painting or sculpture, whether and when it was "published" is legally unsettled. Any US verdict that rests on those questions is marked (inferred). EU: life + 70, so an artist who died before 1956 is PD.

## Wikidata data-quality traps seen (not image-related)

- Q138581656 *Tête Dada*: height recorded as 2943 cm; the true height is about 29 cm (inferred).
- Q19884054 (Taeuber-Arp, MoMA): P6216 holds both "public domain" and "copyrighted".
- Q18927491 (Robert Delaunay, 1938): P6216 says "public domain", which is wrong for the US.
- Q2990594 *Lobster Telephone*: one item for about 11 copies, with only one collection (Mia).
- Escher items mix work and impression: Q2899660 and Q2635136 carry the Israel Museum as collection and also hold the enwiki sitelink.
- Q3212012 *Prose du Transsibérien* is typed as a literary work (the poem), not the artwork.

## Per artist: catalogue raisonné and rights

**Robert Delaunay**
- Catalogue raisonné: the standard one is Guy Habasque's, in Francastel (ed.), *Du cubisme à l'art abstrait* (SEVPEN, 1957), print only ([BnF record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb37435726g)). Richard Riss published a catalogue raisonné of the Eiffel Tower paintings in 2008 (print). **Online catalogue with images: not found.**
- Rights: Pracusa S.A. manages the rights to Sonia, Robert and Charles Delaunay. Robert's works are EU PD (since 2012), so this matters only for the US, and only for post-1930 works. Source: search summary of a AAM community thread, and Thyssen credit lines (inferred).

**Sonia Delaunay**
- Catalogue raisonné: **online catalogue raisonné: not found.**
- Rights: Pracusa S.A. ("Pracusa S.A. manages the rights for works of Sonia, Robert, and Charles Delaunay, and any request for consultation, reproduction, or publication must be submitted to their agreement"). Source: search summary; the Thyssen credits "© Sonia Delaunay Pracusa S.A." (https://www.museothyssen.org/en/collection/artists/delaunay-sonia/simultaneous-contrasts). In the US, ARS is the usual agent (inferred).

**Robert + Sonia jointly**
- No co-attributed work on Wikidata (SPARQL for both as P170: 0 results).
- Their real collaborations were commissions, not attributed objects:
  - the 1937 Exposition decorations (Palais de l'Air and Pavillon des Chemins de fer, architect Félix Aublet). Sonia painted her own panels there, e.g. *Portugal*, 1937, Q131580184, CNAP/MNAM, 645 × 470 cm.
  - the 1938 Salon des Tuileries panels (row 7).
- The famous "joint" work, *La Prose du Transsibérien*, is Sonia with Cendrars (row 8).

**Aleksandra Exter**
- Catalogue raisonné: the Association Alexandra Exter site, https://alexandra-exter.net/en/, has Works and Bibliography sections and authentication warnings, but **no online catalogue raisonné with images was found**. Andréi Nakov holds her moral rights and archive and has published a multivolume catalogue raisonné (print).
- Reproduction policy: not found (there is an unread "Terms of use" link).
- Rights: EU PD (d. 1949). US PD for pre-1931 works.

**Sophie Taeuber-Arp**
- Catalogue raisonné: an online catalogue raisonné database exists, https://sophietaeuberarp.org/english/, published by Stiftung Arp e.V. with the Gerhard-Marcks-Haus, Bremen. Its about-page says "The majority of illustrations are sourced from the image archive established by the Stiftung Arp e.V." and "Photographic credits are placed directly below the illustrated work, stating the photographer, the copyright or the image source" (https://sophietaeuberarp.org/english/catalogue/ueber-den-werkkatalog/). Its usage terms are on a separate Legal Notice page, not read.
- A printed and digital catalogue raisonné project is under way with SIK-ISEA.
- Rights: EU PD (d. 1943). Hauser & Wirth represents the estate.

**M. C. Escher**
- Online gallery: https://mcescher.com/gallery/ (The M.C. Escher Company B.V.). Not a catalogue raisonné.
- Policy: "Any reproduction of his work, including downloading, is prohibited without the express written permission of The M.C. Escher Company" (https://mcescher.com/copyright/). No personal-use allowance is stated.
- Rights: The M.C. Escher Company, which asserts rights worldwide, including over works that are US PD by date (row 15).

**Kay Sage**
- Catalogue raisonné: *Kay Sage: Catalogue Raisonné*, ed. Jessie Sentivan (Delmonico/Prestel, 2018, under Hollis Taggart and Mark Kelman), print only. **Online catalogue: not found.**
- Rights: Estate of Kay Sage / Artists Rights Society (ARS), New York. Inferred from a search summary of museum credit lines, not checked against a credit line itself.

**Salvador Dalí**
- Catalogue raisonné: the online *Catalogue Raisonné of Paintings* by the Fundació Gala-Salvador Dalí, https://catalogues.salvador-dali.org/catalogues/en/catalogue-raisonne-paintings/, free and open (launched 2004, a work in progress).
- Policy (https://www.salvador-dali.org/en/dali-foundation/services/rights-and-image/): "Requests for reproductions of the artist's works should be addressed to VEGAP…" and "it is essential to have the authorization of the Foundation, the institution that manages the artist's industrial and intellectual property".
- Rights: Fundació Gala-Salvador Dalí through VEGAP, and ARS in the US (inferred).

**Alexander Calder**
- Catalogue: https://calder.org (Calder Foundation) has an archive of works. A catalogue raisonné is in preparation (inferred), and **no published online catalogue raisonné was found**.
- Policy (https://calder.org/contact/rights-and-reproduction/): "All requests concerning the use of images of Calder and his works must be addressed to a copyright society representing the Calder Foundation". For high-resolution images it refers people to Art Resource.
- Rights: ARS (USA), ADAGP (France), DACS (UK), VEGAP (Spain), Copyright Agency (Australia).
